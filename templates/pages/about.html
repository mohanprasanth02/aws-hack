AquaGuard AI — Environmental Intelligence Architecture

Solution Blueprint for Challenge PS-054 • Zero-Hardware Water Intelligence

1. PROBLEM STATEMENT (PS-054)

"Build a dashboard that analyzes manually uploaded meter records, identifies unusual consumption patterns and explains possible causes without hardware integration."

2. HARDWARE-FREE DATA PIPELINE ARCHITECTURE

AquaGuard AI eliminates the need for expensive flow meters and wireless IoT telemetry nodes by transforming standard facility management spreadsheets into an actionable intelligence system:

INGEST (.csv/.xlsx) ➔ DATA HEALTH AUDIT ➔ FEATURE ENGINEERING ➔ 7-DAY ROLLING ENVELOPES ➔ MULTI-ALGORITHM DETECTION ➔ ROOT CAUSE HYPOTHESES ➔ GEOSPATIAL SURVEILLANCE ➔ WHAT-IF SIMULATION ➔ REPORTLAB PDF AUDIT

3. HYBRID ENSEMBLE METHODOLOGIES

  • 7-Day Rolling Baseline: Dynamic temporal window mean and standard deviation per meter.
  • Robust Z-Score: Departure magnitude safely handling zero-variance floors.
  • Tukey IQR Fences: Non-parametric quartile ranges resistant to heavy skew.
  • Isolation Forest: Unsupervised tree partitioning across multi-lag volume vectors.
  • AquaGuard Risk Score (0-100): Calibrated weighted composite synthesizing statistical confidence, departure percentage, and duration persistence.
